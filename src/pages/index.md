Hi, I’m George — a computer science researcher and engineer.

My interests include Natural Language Processing, Artificial General Intelligence, and cross-disciplinary AI that bridges human and computational reasoning.

I was recently a post-doctoral researcher at [Birkbeck, University of London](https://bbk.ac.uk), where I worked as a data scientist in the [Mapping Museums Lab](https://mapping-museums.bbk.ac.uk/).

Before that, I completed a PhD in Natural Language Processing at [Queen Mary University of London](http://www.qmul.ac.uk), where I worked on symbolic models of narrative generation. You can watch me talk about my work at [AGI-23 in Stockholm](https://www.youtube.com/live/0CqgXWLHfeI?feature=share&t=24499).
